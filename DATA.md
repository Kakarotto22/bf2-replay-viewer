# What a `.bf2demo` contains

Generated by `scripts/build_data_doc.py` from `bf2demo/inventory.py` and the samples; do not edit by hand.

Time: the simulation runs at 30 ticks per second (`bf2demo/timeline.py`). Print what one demo holds with `python -m bf2demo DEMO --inventory`.

## Data kinds

### Demo header

* **Source:** file header
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** engine version, recording time, server name, map name
* **Read it with:** `--report overview`


### Server settings

* **Source:** DataBlock kind 0 (ServerInfo)
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** name, welcome message, rounds per map, time limit, spawn time, ticket/team ratio, vehicles on/off, voting, team-kill punishment, cameras, radio spam limits, voip ports and quality, co-op bots
* **Read it with:** `--report settings, --export-json`
* **Note:** never the server IP, the admin or rcon password, or the sv.password

### Current map

* **Source:** DataBlock kind 2 (MapInfo), kind 5 (MapList)
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** mode, level, max players, commander enabled, the whole map rotation and the current position in it
* **Read it with:** `--report overview, --map-info`


### Round schedule

* **Source:** BeginRound, EndOfRound, PostRemote game status
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** round start, scheduled end tick (time limit), game status changes, winner at the end
* **Read it with:** `--report overview/timeline`
* **Note:** the recorder stops when the round ends, so EndOfRound is never stored (3 of 92 two-day recordings end with a team at 5 tickets or less); the report infers the ending from the last tickets and flags

### Players

* **Source:** CreatePlayer, DestroyPlayer, ChangePlayerName, KickBan, Player ghost
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** slot id, name (and renames), bot or human, team and team changes, squad, kit, score, kills, deaths, team kills, ping, rank, medals placement counts, commander flag, connected/left times, kicks and bans
* **Read it with:** `--scoreboard, --report players, --export-json`
* **Note:** the wire carries no IP address, CD-key hash or profile id; only the name and the slot id

### Tickets

* **Source:** ScoreManager ghost
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** tickets of both teams over time
* **Read it with:** `--report teams`


### Kills and deaths

* **Source:** KilledBy, PostRemote (death, score awards)
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** killer, victim, weapon or vehicle, assists, suicides, team kills
* **Read it with:** `--feed, --scoreboard`


### Control points

* **Source:** CreateObject (ControlPoint), ControlPoint ghost
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** name, exact position, owner at the start and every change of owner, capture progress, flag raised for which team
* **Read it with:** `--map-info, --report flags`
* **Note:** no capture radius, capture times or controlPointId: those are in the server's GamePlayObjects.con only

### World objects

* **Source:** CreateObject, DestroyObject, join snapshot
* **Exactness:** snapshot
* **Contains:** every vehicle, emplacement, soldier body, projectile, destructible: template, team, position, rotation, lifetime
* **Read it with:** `--export-json, --tracks`
* **Note:** exact spawn transform only for objects created during the recording; objects in the join snapshot carry their current transform (a vehicle may have moved or settled)

### Movement tracks

* **Source:** ghost updates of soldiers, vehicles, projectiles
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** position and rotation samples over time, owner player, distance travelled
* **Read it with:** `--tracks, --export-tracks, --export-json --json-tracks`


### Soldier state

* **Source:** Soldier ghost
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** pose (stand/crouch/prone), aim angles, yaw/pitch, velocity, sprint, swimming, sky diving, weapon in hand, ladder and zipline use, ragdoll bones after death
* **Read it with:** `--export-json --json-tracks`


### Vehicle element state

* **Source:** PlayerControlObject ghost bodies (turrets, rotors, wings, engines, springs)
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** turret and gun angles, rotor and control-surface angles, engine values, landing gear, in physical units
* **Read it with:** `--export-parts, --export-json --json-parts`


### Who sat where

* **Source:** EnterVehicle, ExitVehicle
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** player enters/leaves an object (a soldier object is entered by its player when created)
* **Read it with:** `--feed-verbose`


### Projectiles

* **Source:** MissileInit, StickyProjectile, GenericProjectile ghost
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** which weapon slot of which player fired which projectile, sticky charges and where they stuck
* **Read it with:** `--report weapons`


### Soldier spawns

* **Source:** CreateKit, CreateSpawnGroup, RemoveSpawnGroup
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** exact spawn position of every soldier (basis of SpawnPoint reconstruction), spawn groups and their minimap cell, which are active
* **Read it with:** `--export-con`
* **Note:** a spawn point is only provable when two soldiers spawned at the identical position

### Kit pickups

* **Source:** HandlePickup, HandleDrop
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** kit picked up or dropped, by whom, where
* **Read it with:** `--events`


### Squads and orders

* **Source:** Player ghost (squad_id, squad_invite_mask), SetSquadLeader, ChangeSquadName, SetPrivateSquad, IssueSquadOrder, SetAcceptOrder, TargetDirection
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** squad membership over time, invitations (bit n = invited by squad n), leaders, private squads, orders and who accepted
* **Read it with:** `--report squads, --feed`


### Commander

* **Source:** Commander, Artillery, PostRemote (UAVVehicleCreated/Destroyed, AddParachuteToDropVehicle), objects uav_pred / supply_crate / parachute
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** commander changes, UAV launches and losses, supply crates and parachute drops, artillery control
* **Read it with:** `--report commander, --feed`


### Votes

* **Source:** Vote
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** kind, caller, subject, result and time remaining
* **Read it with:** `--feed`


### Ranks, medals and unlocks

* **Source:** Rank, Medal, Unlock
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** rank ups, medals earned, unlock choices
* **Read it with:** `--events`


### Voice chat activity

* **Source:** VoipOnOff, VoipPlayerMute, VoipSession
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** who started and stopped talking, who muted whom (never the audio)
* **Read it with:** `--events`


### Radio commands and HUD messages

* **Source:** RadioMessage, PostRemote
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** radio command messages and the HUD messages of the server
* **Read it with:** `--feed`
* **Note:** free-text chat is not among the 63 game event classes (it uses a separate message layer); StringBlock is a data block like DataBlock and is in no sample

### Networked static objects

* **Source:** CreateObject (DestroyableObject)
* **Exactness:** snapshot
* **Contains:** destructible bridges, crates, barrels, doors that the server announces
* **Read it with:** `--export-static`


### Environment

* **Source:** AmbientEffectArea, SetNightVision, PlayerTearGassed
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** smoke and gas areas, night vision, tear gas
* **Read it with:** `--events`


### Grappling hook

* **Source:** GrapplingHook* events
* **Exactness:** exact
* **Contains:** rope creation, points and container position
* **Read it with:** `--events`


### Reconstructed GamePlayObjects.con

* **Source:** all of the above
* **Exactness:** derived
* **Contains:** flags, vehicle spawners and spawn points of the recorded map as a server GamePlayObjects.con
* **Read it with:** `--export-con`
* **Note:** see DATA.md, 'Never in a demo' for what the file can only copy from the original map

## Never in a demo

* **Player IP addresses, CD-key hashes, profile ids** - the server never sends them; CreatePlayer has name and slot id only
* **Server IP, rcon/admin password, sv.password** - not part of the ServerInfo block; the voip address field is always 1
* **CombatArea (map bounds)** - created only by the server's .con (CombatAreaManager::createCombatArea); the polygon is never sent. Only its effect is visible: each player's `time_outside_world` (Player ghost) while outside it
* **controlPointId, flag radius, capture times, unableToChangeTeam** - server-side template properties, not replicated
* **Vehicle spawner delays and min/max respawn times** - ObjectSpawners are server-only; only the vehicles they create cross the wire
* **SpawnPoint rotation** - a soldier spawns with the point's position only (CreateKit has no rotation)
* **Exact position of a vehicle that existed before the recording began** - the join snapshot sends the current transform; a fresh vehicle settles by a fixed offset (see spawn-position notes)
* **Static objects that are not networked (buildings, trees, terrain)** - they live in the client's level files
* **Free-text chat, voice audio** - chat travels on a separate message layer (ServerStringMessageEW / StringReceivedEvent); none of the 63 game event classes, all decoded, carries chat text and no sample has any. Voice is only on/off (VoipOnOff)

## Events never seen - and where the same information arrives instead

* `JoinSquad`, `LeaveSquad`, `Invite`: squad membership is the `squad_id` of the Player ghost, invitations its `squad_invite_mask` (`--report squads`).
* `UAV`, `SupplyDrop`, `DropVehicle`: UAVs arrive as `PostRemote` UAVVehicleCreated/Destroyed and `uav_pred` objects, supply crates as `supply_crate` objects, vehicle drops as `parachute` objects and AddParachuteToDropVehicle (`--report commander`).
* `Spotted`, `RadioMessage`: spotting and the medic/ammo/repair/revive calls arrive as `PostRemote` HUD events (`--report radio`); the `RadioMessage` event itself is never stored.
* `Rank`: the rank is in the Player ghost. `EndOfRound`: the recorder stops when the round ends, so it is never stored (the report infers the ending).
* `KickBan`, `ChangePlayerName`, `Unlock`, `CommanderCam`, `ToggleFreeCamera`, `VoipPlayerMute`, `VoipSession`, `ContentCheck`, `PythonCommand`, `Request`, `StringBlock`, `TargetDirection`, `UpdateTrigger`, `PlayerTearGassed`, `SetNightVision`, the grappling hook events and the connection setup events: not in any sample (layout from the binary only).
